🚌 Drive your TN Bus

On Android, turn your phone sideways. Hold ◀ / ▶ to steer, GO to accelerate, STOP to brake. On a keyboard use W/A/S/D or arrow keys and Space. Tap inside the game if keyboard controls do not respond.

The simulator appears after the first successful main-branch Web Export workflow publishes it. If you see a missing page, check the GitHub Actions build. Large downloads may take time on mobile data.
